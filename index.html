NellyGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

NellyGo is the appointment app for Nelly's Salon in Baybayin, Los Baños, Laguna, Philippines. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can have it deleted. It applies to the NellyGo app for Android and iOS.

Information we collect

We only collect what the salon needs to book and manage your appointments.

InformationWhy we use it
Name, email address and phone number To create your account, identify your bookings, and let the salon contact you about an appointment. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account.
Password To sign you in. It is handled by Firebase Authentication. The salon never sees it.
Appointments and waitlist entries The service, date, time, add-ons, prices and status of each booking, plus any notes you type for the salon. Needed to schedule and prepare your visit.
Hair profile (optional) Preferred styles, hair texture and scalp sensitivity, if you fill them in, so the stylist can prepare for your visit.
Saved addresses (optional) Addresses you choose to save in your profile.
Payment records The amount, payment method and reference number of in-store payments, recorded by the salon. Payments are made in the salon. The app does not process payments or collect card or bank details.
In-app alerts Messages about booking confirmations, changes and cancellations.
Push token An ID for each phone you are logged in on, so alerts reach you as push notifications even when the app is closed. It is removed when you log out.
Salon notes Private notes the salon writes about your visits. Only the salon can see them.

Your language and alert settings are stored only on your phone.

NellyGo does not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

How your information is shared

  • The salon (Nelly's Salon) sees your account details and bookings so it can serve you.
  • Other clients can see that a time slot is taken, but never who booked it.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Third-party services

NellyGo uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions and Firebase Cloud Messaging) to sign you in, store the information above and send you alerts, and Google Sign-In if you choose to sign in with Google. Google processes this data on our behalf. See Firebase's privacy and security information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data security

All data sent between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and Google encrypts it where it is stored. Access rules in our database let each client read only their own information. Only the salon can see all bookings and client records.

Deleting your account and data

In the app: open the menu, then Account Settings, then Delete My Account, and confirm with your password or Google account. This:

  • cancels your upcoming appointments and removes your waitlist entries,
  • deletes your in-app alerts, your push tokens, your profile (including hair profile and saved addresses) and your login.

Without the app: email us at tristantib123@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will delete it within 30 days.

What we keep: records of past appointments and payments, and the salon's notes about your visits, are kept as the salon's business records. You can ask us to delete these too by email.

Children's privacy

NellyGo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
